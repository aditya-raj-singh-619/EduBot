## Introduction

National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology (NIELIT), (erstwhile
DOEACC Society), is an Autonomous Scientific Society under the administrative
control of the Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY),
Government of India. It was set up to carry out Human Resource Development and
related activities in the area of Information, Electronics & Communications
Technology (IECT). NIELIT is engaged in both Formal and Non-Formal Education in
IECT, alongside the development of industry-oriented quality education and
training programmes in state-of-the-art areas. NIELIT has worked to establish
itself as the country's premier institution for Examination and Certification
in the field of IECT, and is one of the National Examination Bodies that
accredits institutes/organizations for conducting IT courses in the non-formal
sector.

As of date, NIELIT operates forty-seven (47) centres located at Agartala,
Aizawl, Ajmer, Alawalpur (Saksharta Kendra), Aurangabad, Bhubaneswar, Calicut,
Chandigarh, Chennai, Chuchuyimlang, Churachandpur, Daman, Delhi, Dibrugarh,
Dimapur, Gangtok, Gorakhpur, Guwahati, Haridwar, Imphal, Itanagar, Jammu,
Jorhat, Kargil, Kohima, Kolkata, Kokrajhar, Kurukshetra, Lakhanpur (Saksharta
Kendra), Leh, Lucknow, Lunglei, Majuli, Mandi, Pasighat, Patna, Pali, Ranchi,
Ropar, Senapati, Shillong, Shimla, Silchar, Srinagar, Tezpur, Tura and Tezu,
with its Headquarters in New Delhi. NIELIT also operates extension centres at
many further locations and is networked across India through about 700+
institutes.

Over the last two decades, NIELIT has built expertise in IT training through a
wide range of courses: 'O' Level (Foundation), 'A' Level (Advance Diploma),
'B' Level (MCA equivalent), 'C' Level (M.Tech level); IT literacy courses such
as CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) and BCC (Basic Computer Course); and
other long-term and short-term non-formal courses such as Information
Security, ITeS-BPO (Customer Care/Banking), Computer Hardware Maintenance
(CHM-O/A level), Bio-Informatics (BI-O/A/B level), and ESDM. NIELIT also
offers high-end Post-Graduate level (M.Tech) courses in areas such as
Electronics Design & Technology and Embedded Systems, in association with
respective state Universities — courses not normally offered by formal-sector
institutions.

NIELIT's activities are further extended through R&D projects, consultancy
services, and turnkey projects in office automation, software development,
and website development. NIELIT is also the nodal implementing agency on
behalf of MeitY for Data Digitization of the population of 15 assigned States
and 2 Union Territories, for the creation of the National Population Register
(NPR) project of the Registrar General of India (RGI). NIELIT is also
executing the Agriculture Census and Input Survey project, involving
tabulation of approximately 10 crore data records. NIELIT has planned a
roadmap for adopting appropriate pedagogy to work towards becoming an
Institute of National Importance.

### NIELIT Buxar Centre

NIELIT has established a fully developed extension centre in Buxar, Bihar.
The centre is fully equipped with classrooms, computer labs, electronic labs,
a library, and other necessary amenities for training. It operates under
NIELIT Patna Centre and alongside NIELIT EC Muzaffarpur within NIELIT's Bihar
network.

## Vision

To be the leader in the development of industry oriented quality education
and training and be the country's premier Institution for examination and
certification in the field of Information, Electronics and Communications
Technology (IECT).

## Mission

To be the single source for quality assurance in computer education among the
nation's non-formal institutes. After turning out competent IT professionals
in large numbers, NIELIT's reach is now being extended to all regions of the
country as well as overseas.

## Objectives

- To generate quality manpower and develop skilled professionals in the area
  of Information, Electronics and Communications Technology (IECT) and allied
  fields, by providing world class education & training and accreditation
  services.
- To provide continuing support to learners and trainers through active
  design & development of innovative curricula and acquisition of content,
  aligned with the dynamically changing IECT landscape.
- To establish a quality system of examination and certification that is
  globally recognised, providing a fair assessment of the competency of
  students.
- To continue to implement the DOEACC Scheme for computer courses, jointly
  developed by AICTE and DIT (now MeitY), in the non-formal sector of IT
  Education and Training.
- To establish standards in the areas of IECT and to develop markets in the
  emerging areas.
- To impart continuing education for up-gradation of knowledge and skills, in
  view of high obsolescence in the area of IECT.
- To provide entrepreneurship development programmes in the area of IECT.
- To develop and promote a culture of market-oriented, industry-aligned
  design and development.
- To purchase, lease, hire, exchange, or otherwise acquire any immovable
  property, and to sell, lease, dispose of, exchange, invest, and deal with
  bonds, moneys, securities, and all kinds of movable or immovable property,
  for any of the objectives for which the Society is established.
- To levy and receive moneys in connection with the Society's activities,
  including grants/loans received from the Government or other sources.
- To carry out all activities incidental or ancillary to achieving the
  objectives of the Society.
