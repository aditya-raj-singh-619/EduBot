## About the Course

The 'O' Level (IT) syllabus Revision 5.1 is the fifth revised version under the DOEACC
Scheme, effective from July 2022. The syllabus is designed to enhance students' skills
to solve problems using Information Technology (IT) tools, with a self-learning approach
that enables learners to update themselves on changing technologies.

## Equivalence (Advance Diploma Level)

'O' Level is equivalent to the Foundation level qualification under NIELIT's
non-formal IT education framework.

## Duration

**540 learning hours** (minimum 1 year after 10+2; minimum 6 months after Graduation)

## Credit Scheme

Based on NCVET guidelines (1 credit = 30 hours, theory + practical):
- Each of the 4 modules: 4 credits each (48 theory + 72 practical hrs)
- Project (PJ1-R5.1): 2 credits (60 hrs)
- **Total Credits: 18**

## Examination Pattern

| Mode | Duration | Questions | Max Marks | Min Pass | Weightage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Theory (Online/OMR) | 2 Hours | 100 MCQ | 100 | 33% | 60% |
| Practical (Demo + Viva) | 3 Hours | 3-4 | Lab: 80, Viva: 20 | 33% | 40% |

Minimum Pass: 50% of total weightage (Theory + Practical) in each module.

## Project

The 'O' Level project is a mandatory component carried out under faculty guidance.
It must be original, of real-life value, and not copied from any source. A Project
Completion Certificate must be submitted in the prescribed format from the Head of
the Institution or Organization. The project is submitted only after clearing all
4 theory papers.

## Module Syllabi

### M1-R5.1 — Information Technology Tools and Network Basics
**Duration:** 120 hrs (Theory: 48 hrs + Practical: 72 hrs)

**Objectives:** After completing the module, the student will be able to acquire
confidence in using computers in Office and General Life; identify basic computer
components and terminology; understand file management; create documents using
word processor, spreadsheet & presentation software; understand computer networks,
browse the Internet, search content, use email and collaborate; use e-Governance
applications; understand Social Networking; use Internet for Digital Financial services;
develop knowledge about FutureSkills and Cyber Security; understand various financial
services and Government schemes.

**Units and Duration:**

| Unit | Topic | Theory (hrs) | Practical (hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Computer | 4 | 6 |
| 2 | Introduction to Operating System | 4 | 6 |
| 3 | Word Processing | 6 | 9 |
| 4 | Spreadsheet | 8 | 12 |
| 5 | Presentation | 6 | 9 |
| 6 | Introduction to Internet and WWW | 6 | 9 |
| 7 | E-mail, Social Networking and e-Governance Services | 6 | 9 |
| 8 | Digital Financial Tools and Applications | 4 | 6 |
| 9 | Overview of FutureSkills & Cyber Security | 4 | 6 |

**Marks Distribution:**
- Units 1-2 (Computer + OS): 10 marks
- Unit 3 (Word Processing): 20 marks
- Unit 4 (Spreadsheet): 20 marks
- Unit 5 (Presentation): 20 marks
- Units 6-7 (Internet + Email/Social/eGov): 20 marks
- Units 8-9 (Digital Finance + FutureSkills/Cyber): 10 marks
- Total: 100 marks

**Detailed Syllabus:**
- (i) Introduction to Computer: Computer and Latest IT gadgets, Evolution of Computers
  & its applications, IT gadgets and their applications, Basics of Hardware and Software,
  CPU, Input devices, Output devices, Computer Memory & storage, Application Software,
  Systems Software, Utility Software, Open source and Proprietary Software, Mobile Apps.
- (ii) Introduction to Operating System: OS Basics, OS for Desktop/Laptop, OS for
  Mobile/Tablets, User Interface, Task Bar, Icons & shortcuts, Running applications, OS
  settings (mouse, date/time, display, programs, printers), File and Folder management,
  file extensions.
- (iii) Word Processing: Word Processing Basics, Title Bar/Menu Bar/Toolbars, Creating/
  Opening/Saving/Closing Documents, Page Setup, Page Layout, Borders, Watermark,
  Print Preview, Printing, PDF saving, Text Creation and Manipulation, Document Creation,
  Editing Text, Text Selection, Cut/Copy/Paste, Font/Color/Style/Size, Alignment, Undo/Redo,
  AutoCorrect, Spelling & Grammar, Find and Replace, Formatting (Styles, Paragraph
  Indentation, Bullets and Numbering, Change case, Header & Footer), Table Manipulation
  (Insert/Draw Table, cell width/height, text alignment, row/column operations, Border/
  Shading), Mail Merge, Table of Contents, Indexes, Adding Comments, Tracking changes,
  Macros.
- (iv) Spreadsheet: Elements of Spreadsheet, Cell Address, Entering Data, Page Setup,
  Printing, Saving, Cell Manipulation (Editing, Formatting, Cut/Copy/Paste, AutoFill,
  Sorting & Filtering, Freezing panes), Formulas (Add/Sub/Multiply/Divide, AutoSum,
  Sum/Count/MAX/MIN/AVERAGE, Sort/Filter/Advanced Filter, Database Functions
  (DSUM, DMIN, DMAX, DCOUNT, DCOUNTA), What-if Analysis, Pivot table, Charts
  (Bar, Column, Pie, Line), Data Validation.
- (v) Presentation: Creating/Opening/Saving Presentations, Inserting & Editing Text,
  Inserting/Deleting Slides, Saving, Manipulating Slides (Table, Pictures, Objects,
  Resizing, Master Slide), Slide Show (Setup, Transitions, Slide Timings, Automating),
  Aesthetics & Printing (Color/Line Style, Movie/Sound, Headers/Footers/Notes, Printing
  Slides and Handouts).
- (vi) Introduction to Internet and WWW: Basic Computer Networks (LAN, WAN,
  Network Topology), Internet (Concept, WWW, Applications, Website Address/URL,
  IP Address, ISP, Internet Protocol, Connectivity modes, IP/MAC/IMEI), Popular Web
  Browsers, Surfing, Search Engines, Downloading, Printing.
- (vii) E-mail, Social Networking and e-Governance Services: Structure of E-mail, Using
  E-mails (Opening account, Inbox/Outbox, Creating/Sending/Replying/Forwarding/
  Searching/Attaching/Email Signature), Social Networking & e-Commerce (Facebook,
  Twitter, LinkedIn, Instagram, WhatsApp, Telegram, Blogs, E-commerce, Netiquettes),
  e-Governance Services (Railway Reservation, Passport, eHospital/ORS, UMANG APP),
  Digital Locker.
- (viii) Digital Financial Tools and Applications: OTP and QR Code, UPI, AEPS, USSD,
  Card (Credit/Debit), eWallet, PoS, Internet Banking (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS), Online
  Bill Payment.
- (ix) Overview of FutureSkills and Cyber Security: IoT, Big Data Analytics, Cloud
  Computing, Virtual Reality, Artificial Intelligence, Social & Mobile, Blockchain
  Technology, 3D Printing/Additive Manufacturing, Robotics Process Automation; Cyber
  Security (Need, Securing PC, Securing Smart Phone).

**Software:** Linux/Ubuntu 16+, LibreOffice 6.0+, Standard Browser

### M2-R5.1 — Web Designing and Publishing
**Duration:** 120 hrs (Theory: 48 hrs + Practical: 72 hrs)

**Objective:** Equip students to design and create web pages using HTML, CSS,
JavaScript and Angular JS; integrate multimedia objects; publish websites.

**Units and Duration:**

| Unit | Topic | Theory (hrs) | Practical (hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Web Design | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | Editors | 2 | 3 |
| 3 | HTML Basics | 10 | 15 |
| 4 | Cascading Style Sheets (CSS) | 10 | 15 |
| 5 | CSS Framework | 6 | 9 |
| 6 | JavaScript and Angular JS | 10 | 15 |
| 7 | Photo Editor | 6 | 9 |
| 8 | Web Publishing and Browsing | 2 | 3 |

**Marks Distribution:**
- Units 1-2 + HTML Basics: 25 marks
- CSS: 20 marks
- CSS Framework: 15 marks
- JavaScript and Angular JS: 20 marks
- Photo Editor + Web Publishing and Browsing: 20 marks
- Total: 100 marks

**Detailed Syllabus:**
- (i) Introduction to Web Design: Internet, WWW, Website, Working of Websites, Webpages,
  Front End, Back End, Client/Server Scripting, Responsive Web Designing, Static and
  Dynamic Websites.
- (ii) Editors: Notepad++, Sublime Text Editor, file creation/editing/saving.
- (iii) HTML Basics: HTML Introduction, Basic Structure, Head Section, Formatting Tags
  (Bold, Italic, Underline, Strikethrough, Div, Pre), Anchor links, Image Tag, Paragraphs,
  Comments, Tables (Border, Cellpadding, Cellspacing, TR/TH/TD, Rowspan/Colspan),
  Lists (Ordered, Unordered, Definition), Forms (Input types, Text Area, Dropdown, Radio,
  Checkboxes, Submit/Reset), Frames; HTML5 (New Elements: Section/Nav/Article/Aside/
  Audio/Video, Form Validations, Multimedia, Layout, Iframe).
- (iv) CSS: Introduction, Types of CSS, Selectors (Universal, ID, Tag, Class, Sub, Attribute,
  Group), CSS Properties (Background, Block, Box, List, Border, Positioning), CSS Lists/
  Tables/Menu Design/Image Gallery.
- (v) CSS Framework: W3.CSS (Intro, Colors, Containers, Panels, Borders, Fonts, Text,
  Tables, List, Images, Grid).
- (vi) JavaScript and Angular JS: Client Side Scripting, Variables, Operators, Conditional
  Statements, Popup Boxes, JS Events, Form Validations. Angular JS: Expressions, Modules
  and Directives.
- (vii) Photo Editor: Selection Tools, Paint Tools, Transform Tools, Text Tool, Layers,
  Brightness/Contrast, Color/Tone, Filters.
- (viii) Web Publishing and Browsing: SGML, Web hosting Basics, Documents Interchange
  Standards, Components of Web Publishing, Document management, Web Page Design
  Considerations, Search/Meta Search Engines, WWW, Browser, HTTP, Publishing Tools.

### M3-R5.1 — Programming and Problem Solving Through Python
**Duration:** 120 hrs (Theory: 48 hrs + Practical: 72 hrs)

**Objective:** Make learners understand programming language concepts: Data Types,
Loops, Functions; Python Lists, Strings, Tuples, Dictionaries, Elementary Data Handling
using Pandas, NumPy etc. Draw flowcharts, develop algorithms, use Python constructs
(conditional, iteration), manage data using NumPy, handle files and create Modules.

**Units and Duration:**

| Unit | Topic | Theory (hrs) | Practical (hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to Programming | 2 | 3 |
| 2 | Algorithm and Flowcharts to Solve Problems | 6 | 9 |
| 3 | Introduction to Python | 2 | 3 |
| 4 | Operators, Expressions and Python Statements | 10 | 15 |
| 5 | Sequence Data Types | 6 | 9 |
| 6 | Functions | 10 | 15 |
| 7 | File Processing | 6 | 9 |
| 8 | Modules | 2 | 3 |
| 9 | NumPy Basics | 4 | 6 |
| **Total** | | **48** | **72** |

**Marks Distribution:**
- Units 1-2 (Programming Intro + Algorithms): 20 marks
- Units 3-5 (Python Intro + Operators + Sequence Types): 30 marks
- Units 6-8 (Functions + File Processing + Modules): 40 marks
- Unit 9 (NumPy Basics): 10 marks
- Total: 100 marks

**Detailed Syllabus:**
- (i) Introduction to Programming: Basic Model of computation, algorithms, flowcharts,
  Programming Languages, compilation, testing & debugging, documentation.
- (ii) Algorithms and Flowcharts: Flowchart Symbols, Basic algorithms for sequential/
  decision-based/iterative processing. Examples: swapping values, summation, base
  conversion, reversing digits, GCD, prime testing, factorial, Fibonacci, sin x series,
  array reversal, finding largest number, upper triangular matrix printing.
- (iii) Introduction to Python: Python Introduction, Technical Strength, Python Interpreter
  and program execution, Comments, Literals, Constants, Built-in Data types: Numbers
  (Integers, Floats, Complex, Real, Sets), Strings (Slicing, Indexing, Concatenation),
  Accepting input, printing statements, simple Python programs.
- (iv) Operators, Expressions and Python Statements: Assignment, expressions, Arithmetic/
  Relational/Logical/Bitwise operators and precedence, Conditional statements (if, if-else,
  if-elif-else), Iterative computation and control flow: range(), While, For, break,
  continue, pass, else, assert.
- (v) Sequence Data Types: Lists, tuples and dictionary (Slicing, Indexing, Concatenation),
  concept of mutability, finding max/min/mean, linear search on list/tuple, counting
  frequency of elements using dictionary.
- (vi) Functions: Top-down approach, Modular approach, in-built functions, user-defined
  functions, Recursive functions, String functions, Numeric Functions: eval(), max(), min(),
  pow(), round(), int(), random(), ceil(), floor(), sqrt(), Date & Time Functions.
- (vii) File Processing: Concept of Files, File opening modes and closing, Reading from a
  file, Writing onto a file, File functions: open(), close(), read(), readline(), readlines(),
  write(), writelines(), tell(), seek(), Command Line arguments.
- (viii) Scope and Modules: Scope of objects and Names, LEGB Rule, Module Basics,
  Module Files as Namespaces, Import Model, Reloading Modules.
- (ix) NumPy Basics: Introduction to NumPy ndarray, datatypes, array attributes, array
  creation routines, Array From Existing Data, Array From Numerical Ranges,
  Indexing & Slicing.

### M4-R5.1 — Internet of Things and its Applications
**Duration:** 120 hrs (Theory: 48 hrs + Practical: 72 hrs)

**Objective:** Equip students to understand IoT basics, interface sensors and actuators
with Arduino, write C programs in Arduino IDE, understand serial communication, build
IoT applications, understand security of IoT devices, and develop soft skills.

**Units and Duration:**

| Unit | Topic | Theory (hrs) | Practical (hrs) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to IoT – Applications/Devices/Protocols/Communication Model | 4 | 6 |
| 2 | Things and Connections | 4 | 6 |
| 3 | Sensors, Actuators and Microcontrollers | 8 | 12 |
| 4 | Building IoT Applications | 20 | 30 |
| 5 | Security and Future of IoT Ecosystem | 4 | 6 |
| 6 | Soft Skills — Personality Development | 8 | 12 |

**Marks Distribution:**
- Unit 1 (IoT Intro/Protocols/Communication): 10 marks
- Unit 2 (Things and Connections): 10 marks
- Unit 3 (Sensors, Actuators, Microcontrollers): 15 marks
- Unit 4 (Building IoT Applications): 40 marks
- Unit 5 (Security and Future of IoT): 5 marks
- Unit 6 (Soft Skills): 20 marks
- Total: 100 marks

**Detailed Syllabus:**
- (i) Introduction to IoT: Overview of IoT, characteristics of devices and applications,
  building blocks, various technologies, IoT levels, IoT design methodology, Physical/
  Logical Design of IoT, Functional blocks and Communication Models, Development
  Tools.
- (ii) Things and Connections: Controlled Systems, Real-time systems with feedback loop,
  TCP/IP vs OSI model, wired and wireless connectivity, process flow of an IoT
  application.
- (iii) Sensors, Actuators and Microcontrollers: Sensors (measuring physical quantities:
  light, moisture, temperature sensors); Actuators (DC motors etc.); Microcontroller
  vs microprocessor, types of microcontrollers.
- (iv) Building IoT Applications: Arduino IDE (writing sketch, compiling, debugging,
  uploading, serial monitor); Embedded C Language (Variables, Built-in Data Types,
  Arithmetic operators, Constants, Assignment); Conditional Statements and Loops (if-else,
  while, do-while, for, Nested/Infinite loops, Switch); Arrays (single dimension); Functions
  (Standard Library, Function prototype, return type, function call); Interfacing sensors
  (digital vs analog pins: LED, Button, Sensors: DHT, LDR, MQ135, IR, LCD, Keypad);
  Serial communication (HC-05 Bluetooth); Controlling 220V AC (relay module).
- (v) Security and Future of IoT: Why Security, Privacy for IoT devices, Security levels,
  Protecting IoT devices; Future: AI/ML penetration to IoT.
- (vi) Soft Skills — Personality Development: Personality development, self-awareness,
  motivation, self-discipline, building positive personality, gestures; Self-esteem:
  self-efficacy, self-motivation, time management, stress management, etiquettes; 
  Communication and writing skills: Resume, Letters, Report, Presentation; Interview
  skills and body language.

**Hardware Requirement:** Computers with standard networking; Arduino UNO or
equivalent board, sensors and motors; NIC, RJ-45 connector, Crimping Tools, UTP/STP/
Coaxial/Fiber Optic cables, 8/16 port Switch, Wi-Fi Router.

**Software Requirement:** Linux/Ubuntu 16+, LibreOffice 6.0+, Python interpreter,
Standard Antivirus, Standard Browser + publishing tools, Arduino IDE (any open source IoT tool).
