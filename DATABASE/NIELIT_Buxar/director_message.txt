Message:
Welcome to the official website of the National Institute of Electronics &
Information Technology (NIELIT), Buxar Centre.

NIELIT Buxar is a centre committed to excellence in digital education, skill
development, and capacity building. As technology continues to reshape the world,
it is essential for individuals to continuously enhance their knowledge and skills
to remain relevant in an increasingly digital society.

NIELIT Buxar strives to create opportunities for learners from all sections of
society by providing quality training in Information Technology, Electronics,
Digital Literacy, Artificial Intelligence, Cyber Security, Internet of Things
(IoT), Data Analytics, and other emerging technologies. The objective is to
empower youth with industry-relevant skills that enhance employability and
support career growth.

The Centre actively contributes to various skill development and capacity-building
initiatives of the Government of India. Through NSQF-aligned courses, digital
literacy programs, and employment-oriented training, NIELIT Buxar aims to bridge
the digital divide and ensure that the benefits of technology reach every corner
of society, particularly rural and underserved communities.
