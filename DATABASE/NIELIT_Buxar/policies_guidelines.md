# NIELIT — Policies & Guidelines

## About This Website

This is the official website of the National Institute of Electronics and
Information Technology (NIELIT), Ministry of Electronics and Information
Technology, Government of India — designed, developed and hosted by NIELIT.

The site has been developed to provide information to the general public. An
attempt has been made through this site to provide reliable, comprehensive,
accurate information about NIELIT and its various Groups/Organizations.
Hyperlinks at various places have been provided to other Indian Government
Portals/Websites.

The content on this site is the result of a collaborative effort of various
Groups and Divisions of the Department. It is NIELIT's endeavour to continue
the enhancement and enrichment of this site in terms of content coverage,
design and technology on a regular basis.
