# NIELIT — Content Contribution, Moderation & Approval Policy (CMAP)

## Overview

Content needs to be contributed by the authorized Content Manager from
various wings of NIELIT in a consistent fashion to maintain uniformity and
to bring in standardization, along with associated metadata and keywords.

The content on the portal goes through the entire life-cycle process, which
consists of:

1. Creation
2. Modification
3. Approval
4. Moderation
5. Publishing
6. Expiry
7. Archival

Once the content is contributed, it needs to be approved and moderated prior
to being published on the website. The moderation could be multilevel and is
role-based. If the content is rejected at any level, it is reverted back to
the originator of the content for modification.

## Content Element Categories

Content elements are categorized by urgency of the update process:

- **Routine** — activities that are done as a normal part of a job or process.
- **Priority** — activities that are done as an urgent part of a job or process.
- **Express** — activities that are done as the most urgent part of a job or process.
