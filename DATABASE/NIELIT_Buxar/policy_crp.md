# NIELIT — Content Review Policy (CRP)

## Overview

The NIELIT website is an important tool for disseminating information to the
masses served by the organization. It is therefore required to keep the
content on the website current and up-to-date, hence the need for the
Content Review Policy. Since the scope of the content is huge, different
review policies are defined for the diverse content elements.

The review policy is based on the type of content element, its validity and
relevance, as well as the archival policy.
