# NIELIT — Recognition

## National Recognition of NIELIT's Non-Formal Courses

NIELIT's non-formal IT courses are recognized at the national level:

- 'O' Level
- 'A' Level
- 'B' Level
- 'C' Level

- CCC (Course on Computer Concepts)
- CCC Plus
