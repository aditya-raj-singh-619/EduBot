# NIELIT — Liaison Officer for SCs/STs/OBCs/PWDs & EWS

**Ref:** No. A-20/1/2020-Admin (E-4196)/85 | **Date:** 02 February 2023
**Type:** Office Memorandum | **Issued by:** Registrar, NIELIT Headquarters

## Nomination

With the approval of the Competent Authority, **Shri Ashwin Pawar, Deputy
Director (Technical)** is nominated as **Liaison Officer** for SCs/STs/OBCs/
PWDs & EWS for NIELIT Headquarters, with immediate effect. This assignment
is in addition to his existing duties and responsibilities.

## Duties of the Liaison Officer

1. Ensure due compliance by subordinate authorities with reservation orders
   and other benefits admissible to SCs/STs/OBCs/PWDs & EWS.
2. Scrutinize and ensure prompt submission of the prescribed annual
   statements by the Appointing Authorities to the Ministry/Department, and
   consolidation of the annual statements.
3. Liaise between the Ministry/Department and Centres for providing
   required information, answering queries and clearing doubts.
4. Conduct annual inspection of the Reservation Registers/Rosters maintained
   by NIELIT Centres and Headquarters.

## Compliance Requirement

While functioning as Liaison Officer for SCs/STs/OBCs/PWDs & EWS, the
nominated officer must ensure compliance with all instructions/orders issued
by the Government of India from time to time.
