# NIELIT — Policy for Opening of New Centres

**Ref:** No. 26(2)2016-NIELIT (Civil)/7059 | **Date:** 08 November 2016
**Type:** Office Memorandum | **Subject:** Policy for opening of new National
Institute of Electronics and Information Technology (NIELIT) Centres
**Issued by:** Registrar, NIELIT

## Background

NIELIT is a premier institution for Training, Examination and Certification
in the IECT area (under the administrative control of the Ministry of
Electronics & Information Technology, MeitY, Government of India), carrying
out capacity building and skill development activities nationwide through a
network of its own Centres/Extension Centres at 35 locations and a wide
network of 900+ accredited institutes and 9,000 facilitation centres.

Looking to requests received from many State Governments for setting up
NIELIT Centres, and with the approval of the Hon'ble Minister of Electronics
& Information Technology, Government of India, and Chairperson, Governing
Council (NIELIT), the following policy has been drawn up for opening new
NIELIT Centres/Extension Centres.

## Policy Parameters

| Parameter | NIELIT Centre | Extension Centre |
|---|---|---|
| **Centres per State** | One per State/UT | 2–3 depending on size and population of State/UT |
| **Location** | In State Capital OR in cities with min. 25 Lakh population | In cities with min. 5 Lakh population |
| **Built-up Space** | 8,000–10,000 sqft, to be provided by State Govt. free of cost | 3,000–4,000 sqft, to be provided by State Govt. free of cost |
| **Permanent Campus** | Only on the basis of performance of the Centre/Extension Centre in the initial 3 years of starting operations from built-up space | Same criterion |
| **Land (if permanent campus to be set up)** | 3–5 Acres of land, free of cost, by State Government | 1–2 Acres, free of cost, by State Government |
| **Location of Land** | Within municipal limits of the city, or within 2 km outside municipal limits, with availability of approach road, electricity and water etc. on site by State Government | Same criterion |
| **Funding Support** | For the initial 3–5 years from DeitY. NIELIT will contribute 10% of recurring cost through revenue generation or from its own corpus during the project implementation period (initial phase) | Same criterion |
| **Transportation** | Public transport to be made available by State Government, or transportation facilities under the project | Same criterion |

## Evaluation Process

A committee evaluates the suitability of a proposed location by considering
factors such as: (i) condition of built-up space & location in the city,
(ii) transport, (iii) literacy level of the region, (iv) approach and
availability of public educational establishments in nearby areas, and (v)
industries in the region.

**Committee Composition:**
- Director General, NIELIT (or nominee) — Chairman
- Nominee of MeitY — Member
- Nominee of State Government or any University/College/Institute in the
  State — Member
- Nominee of Technical Division, NIELIT HQ — Member Secretary

Based on the committee's report, NIELIT submits a proposal to MeitY for
funding support and further action.
