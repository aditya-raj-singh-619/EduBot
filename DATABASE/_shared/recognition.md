# NIELIT — Recognition of Courses

## National Recognition of NIELIT's Non-Formal Courses

NIELIT's non-formal IT education courses are nationally recognized at the
following levels:

- **O Level** (Foundation level)
- **A Level** (Advance Diploma level)
- **B Level** (MCA equivalent level)
- **C Level** (M.Tech equivalent level)

## Recognition of Digital Literacy Courses by State Governments / UT Administrations

NIELIT's digital literacy courses are recognized by various State Governments
and Union Territory Administrations:

- **ACC** — Advanced Course on Computer Concepts
- **BCC** — Basic Course on Computer Concepts
- **CCC** — Course on Computer Concepts
- **CCC Plus** — Course on Computer Concepts Plus
- **ECC** — Elementary Certificate Course (on Computer Concepts)

Source: Official NIELIT website, "Recognition" page. This is an HQ-level
