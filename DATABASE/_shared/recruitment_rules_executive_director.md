# NIELIT — Revised Recruitment Rules for the Post of Executive Director

**Ref:** No. NIELIT/HQ/EST/54/09(II) Pt./317 | **Date:** 14 February 2017
**Type:** Office Memorandum | **Issued by:** Registrar, NIELIT

## Background

To meet the challenges of new technologies and to have employees with a good
academic record — so as to impart skill training and capacity building etc.
— a proposal to revise the Recruitment Rules for the posts of Executive
Director (PB-4: Rs.37,400–67,000/- with GP of Rs.10,000/-) and Director
(PB-4: Rs.37,400–67,000/- with GP of Rs.8,900/-) was placed before the
Management Board of NIELIT in its 12th meeting held on 26 February 2016.

After approval from the Management Board and with the advice of ABC Div.,
MeitY, the matter was placed before the Governing Council, NIELIT, in its
34th meeting held on 21 December 2016, for ratification. The Governing
Council ratified the proposal, and the Recruitment Rules for the following
posts were notified:

| Sr.No. | Name of the Post | Pay Scale | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive Director | PB-4: Rs.37,400–67,000/- + GP Rs.10,000/- | Annexure 'A' |
| 2 | Director | PB-4: Rs.37,400–67,000/- + GP Rs.8,900/- | Annexure 'B' |

*(This notice issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.)*

## Recruitment Rules — Executive Director (Annexure 'A')

- **Number of Posts:** 8 (subject to variation dependent on workload)
- **Classification:** Not Applicable
- **Scale of Pay:** Pay Band & Grade Pay — PB4: 37,400–67,000 + Grade Pay Rs.10,000/-
- **Selection or Non-Selection:** Not applicable
- **Age Limit for Direct Recruits:** Up to 50 years (relaxation as per GOI Rules)
  - *Note:* The crucial date for determining the age limit shall be the closing date for receipt of applications from candidates in India, and not the closing date prescribed for those in Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, Mizoram, Manipur, Nagaland, Tripura, Sikkim, Ladakh Division and Pangi Sub-division of HP, Andaman & Nicobar Islands and Lakshadweep.

### Educational and Other Qualifications for Direct Recruits

(a) BE/B.Tech in Computer Science/Information Technology/Electrical/
Electronics/Electronics & Communications/Telecommunications with minimum
60% marks from a recognized University/Institution, or M.Sc. in Physics/
Electronics/Applied Electronics Information Technology/Computer Science
with minimum 60% marks from a recognized University/Institution — with 20
years' experience.
**OR**
(b) M.Tech/ME in Computer Science/Information Technology/Electricals/
Electronics/Electronics & Communications/Telecommunications with minimum
60% marks from a recognized University/Institution — with 17 years'
experience.
**OR**
(c) PhD in any of the above-cited subjects — with 12 years' experience.

*Whether age and other educational qualifications prescribed for direct
recruits will apply in the case of promotees:* Not applicable.

### Recruitment and Promotion Details

- **Period of Probation:** One year in the case of Direct Recruitment
- **Methods of Recruitment:** By Direct Recruitment/Transfer (Absorption),
  failing which by Deputation
- **In case of recruitment by promotion/deputation/absorption:** Officers of
  the Central/State Government/PSUs/Autonomous Bodies —
  (i) holding an analogous post on regular basis, **OR** having 5 years'
  regular service in PB4: 37,400–67,000 with GP Rs.8,900/-, **AND**
  (ii) possessing the qualifications prescribed for Direct Recruitment as
  above.
- **Departmental Promotion Committee / Search-cum-Selection Committee
  Composition:**
  - Secretary, MeitY — Chairperson
  - An Eminent Scientist — Member
  - Secretary of another scientific department — Member
  - An outside expert from the relevant field — Member
  - Director General, NIELIT — Member
- **Circumstances for UPSC Consultation:** Not applicable
