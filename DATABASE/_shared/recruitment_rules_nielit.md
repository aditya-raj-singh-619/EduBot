NIELIT

## Background

Per a letter from DeitY (No. 7(1)/2015-ABCD dated 13 March 2015), forwarding
a communication from the Ministry of Personnel, P.G. & Pensions, Deptt. of
Personnel & Training (No. AB-14017/61/2008-Estt.(RR), dated 27 February
2015), DoPT directed all Ministries/Departments to upload notified
Recruitment Rules (RRs) of various posts on their official websites.

The RRs for S&T and Non-S&T staff of NIELIT were duly approved in the
31st/32nd Governing Council meetings held on 8 October 2013 and 29 November
2014 respectively (Agenda Item No. 32.2). Additional RRs were drafted and
approved by DeitY, and others were approved by the Managing Committee &
Information Technology (MC&IT), as summarized below.

### Scientist/Engineer-D / Joint Director
- **Posts:** 22 | **Classification:** Group 'A' | **Pay:** PB3: 15,600–39,100 + GP Rs.7600 | **Mode:** Selection | **Age:** Up to 40 years
- **Essential Qualification:** (a) Regular BE/B.Tech in Computer Science/Computer Engg./IT/Electrical & Electronics Engg./Electronics & Instrumentation/Electronics/Electronics & Communications, or M.Sc (Electronics/Applied Electronics/Physics), First Class, **OR** (b) M.Tech/ME in Computer Science/IT/Electronics & Communications/Electronics or a specialization (VLSI, Information Security, Embedded Systems, Software Engineering, AI, Networking, etc.), First Class, **OR** (c) PhD in Computer Engg./Computer Science/IT/Electronics and allied fields.
- **Experience:** 8 years for (a), 6 years for (b), 4 years for (c)
- **Probation:** One year for direct recruits | **Recruitment:** Direct Recruitment/Absorption, failing which by Deputation
- **Selection Committee:** Managing Director (HQ)/Centre Director (Centres) — Chairman; two S&T Officers from DeitY (Scientist 'F' or above) — Members; two outside experts (GP Rs.8900+) — Members. One member must be from Reserved Category (SC/ST).

- **Posts:** 1 (2005) | **Classification:** Group 'A' | **Pay:** Rs.18,400-500-22,400 | **Age:** Up to 55 years

## Norms for Administrative/Finance Posts — Registrar (Annexure-I)

post at three pay-scale tiers:

| Pay Scale | Age (Direct Recruitment) | Essential Qualification & Experience |
|---|---|---|

| Rs.10,000–15,200 | Not exceeding 50 years | Graduate + PG Diploma in Management; 10 years' experience |

Ministry of Information Technology/other Ministries/Depts./Govt.
organizations, or persons working in the immediately lower pay scale with 5
years' relevant experience.
