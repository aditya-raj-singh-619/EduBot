# ACC — Awareness in Computer Concepts

## Objective

By undergoing the Awareness in Computer Concepts (ACC) course, a learner
should be able to acquire basic knowledge on computers and their usage by
understanding the following skills:

- How to operate the elements of a computer
- Perform basic operations on the computer
- Create, edit and format documents using a word processor
- Access the Internet and find information of interest
- Register for a web-based e-mail account and use it, including opening
  e-mail attachments
- Understand Financial Literacy
- Understand the concept of Online Banking (Digital Literacy)
- Understand the available e-Governance Services

The module on financial literacy enables individuals to understand various
financial services and become aware of various Government of India schemes.

## Course Duration

20 hours total — Theory: 9 hours + Practical: 11 hours

## Eligibility

No minimum qualification is required for applying and appearing for the
examination in the Awareness in Computer Concepts (ACC) course.
