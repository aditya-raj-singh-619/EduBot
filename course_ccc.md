# CCC — Course on Computer Concepts

**Revision:** 4 | **Effective:** 01 October 2023
**Issued by:** National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology (NIELIT)

---

## Objective

The course is designed to equip a person to use computers for professional as
well as day to day use. It provides theoretical background as well as
in-depth knowledge of software/packages. After completing the course, the
incumbent will be digitally literate and will be able to:

- Acquire confidence in using computers in Office and General Life
- Identify the basic components of computers and terminology
- Understand file management
- Create documents using word processor, spreadsheet & presentation software
- Understand computer networks, and browse the internet, content search,
  email and collaborate with peers
- Use e-Governance applications; and use computer to improve existing skills
  and learn new skills
- Understand Social Networking platforms
- Use internet for Digital Financial services
- Develop introductory knowledge of Cyber Security
- Develop knowledge about Future skills

The module on financial literacy enables individuals to understand various
financial services and become aware of various Government of India schemes.

## Duration

**90 Hours** — Theory: 30 hrs + Practical: 60 hrs

This course can also be offered as a 10 days' full-time intensive course.

## Eligibility

No minimum qualification is required for applying and appearing for the
examination in Course on Computer Concepts (CCC).

## Job Role

Computer Operator, Data Entry Operator and Social Media Operator

### Chapter 1 — Introduction to Computer
Computer and Latest IT gadgets (evolution of computers & applications, IT
gadgets); Basics of Hardware and Software — CPU, input/output devices,
computer memory & storage; Application, Systems and Utility Software; Open
source vs Proprietary Software; Mobile Apps.
**Outcome:** Identify computers/IT gadgets and their evolution; familiarity
with input, output and hardware components along with storage devices; know
various software types and mobile apps.

### Chapter 2 — Introduction to Operating System
Basics of OS; OS for Desktop/Laptop and for Mobile/Tablets; User Interface
(Task Bar, Icons & Shortcuts, Running an Application); OS simple settings
(mouse properties, system date/time, display properties, add/remove
programs, adding/removing/sharing printers); File and Folder Management;
Types of file extensions.
**Outcome:** Acquainted with OS and its applications on desktop and mobile;
able to identify screen components, modify system settings, manage files and
folders, print, and recognize file extensions.

### Chapter 3 — Word Processing
Word Processing Basics (opening the package, Title/Menu Bar, Toolbars &
Sidebar, creating a document); Opening/Closing Documents (save/save as, help,
page setup, print preview, printing, saving as PDF); Text Creation &
Manipulation (editing, selection, cut/copy/paste, font/color/style/size,
alignment, undo/redo, autocorrect/spelling/grammar, find & replace);
Formatting (indentation, bullets/numbering, change case, header & footer);
Table Manipulation (insert/draw, resize, alignment, insert/delete/merge/split
cells, border & shading); Mail Merge; Shortcut Keys.
**Outcome:** In-depth knowledge of word processing; open/save/print documents
including PDFs; create/format documents, insert headers/footers, find &
correct text, work with tables, and use Mail Merge.

### Chapter 4 — Spread Sheet
Elements of Spread Sheet (creating, cell address, entering data, page setup,
printing, saving, opening/closing); Manipulation of Cells & Sheet (editing
content, formatting, cut/copy/paste special, resizing, insert/delete
rows-columns, AutoFill, sorting & filtering, freezing panes); Formulas,
Functions and Charts (basic arithmetic formulas, AutoSum, SUM/COUNT/MAX/MIN/
AVERAGE, Bar/Pie/Line charts).
**Outcome:** Create, edit, sort and filter spreadsheet data; apply formulas
and functions; prepare charts to represent information pictorially.

### Chapter 5 — Presentation
Creation of Presentation (template-based, blank, inserting/editing text,
inserting/deleting slides, saving); Manipulating Slides (tables, clip art,
other objects, resizing/scaling, Master Slide); Presentation of Slides
(set-up, running slide show, transitions & timings, automating); Aesthetics &
Printing (text enhancement, color/line style, movie/sound, headers/footers/
notes, printing slides and handouts).
**Outcome:** Create and run presentations; manipulate slides with pictures,
objects and multimedia; run slide shows with transitions.

### Chapter 6 — Introduction to Internet and WWW
Basics of Computer Networks (LAN, WAN, Network Topology); Internet (concept
of Internet & WWW, applications, website address & URL, IP address, ISP &
its role, Internet Protocol, connection modes — Hotspot/Wi-Fi/LAN/Broadband/
USB Tethering, identifying IP/MAC/IMEI); Popular Web Browsers; Exploring the
Internet (surfing, search engines, searching, downloading and printing web
pages).
**Outcome:** Knowledge of network types/topologies; overview of Internet and
browsers; connect via various modes; identify devices on local/Internet
networks; search, download and print web content.

### Chapter 7 — E-mail, Social Networking and e-Governance Services
Structure of E-mail; Using E-mails (opening account, inbox/outbox,
composing/sending, replying, forwarding, searching, attachments, signature);
Social Networking & e-Commerce (Facebook/Twitter/LinkedIn/Instagram, Instant
Messaging — WhatsApp/Messenger/Telegram, Blogs, basics of e-Commerce,
Netiquettes); e-Governance Services overview (Railway Reservation, Passport,
eHospital/ORS); UMANG App; Digital Locker.
**Outcome:** Create and use email with attachments; use social networking,
messaging and blogs; access e-Governance, e-Commerce and mobile apps.

### Chapter 8 — Digital Financial Tools and Applications
Digital Financial Tools (OTP, QR Code, UPI, AEPS, USSD, Credit/Debit Card,
eWallet, PoS); Internet Banking (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS); Online Bill Payment.
**Outcome:** Know digital financial tools and internet banking modes; use
e-Governance/e-Commerce/mobile apps; use Digital Locker to store documents.

### Chapter 9 — Overview of Cyber Security
Need and Goal of Cyber Security; Securing PC; Securing Browser; Securing
Email and Social Media Accounts (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp); Securing
Smart Phone.
**Outcome:** Understand the need for cyber security; secure PC and mobile
devices using basic security features.

### Chapter 10 — Overview of Future Skills and Artificial Intelligence
Introduction to Future Skills — IoT, Big Data Analytics, Cloud Computing,
Virtual Reality, Artificial Intelligence, Social & Mobile, Blockchain
Technology, 3D Printing/Additive Manufacturing, Robotics Process Automation;
What is AI, History, Why AI, Goals, Ethics, Advantages/Disadvantages,
Applications, Types, Subsets, Future of AI, AI at Present, Myths about
Advanced AI, Future impact of AI across sectors.
**Outcome:** Familiarity with latest trends/technologies in upcoming IECT
fields; understanding of AI's present and future use across sectors.

## Practical Software Note

For practical purposes, the latest version of Free Open Source Ubuntu &
LibreOffice may be used.
