# NIELIT — Content Archival Policy (CAP)

## Overview

The Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW) stipulate that expired
content must not be presented or flashed on the website. Therefore, as per
the content archival policy adopted by this department, content will be
deleted from the site after its expiry date. Important data will be shifted
to the archives page. Content contributors should revalidate/modify content
periodically to ensure that expired data is not present/flashed on the site.
Wherever content is no longer needed to be displayed, suitable advice may be
sent to the Web Information Manager for its archival/deletion.

Each content component is accompanied by metadata, source and a validity
date. For some components the validity date may not be known — i.e., the
content is stated to be perpetual. In this scenario, the validity date
should be **ten years**.

For a few components, such as announcements and tenders, only live content
whose validity date is after the current date is shown on the website. For
other components — documents, schemes, services, forms, websites and the
contact directory — there is a need for timely review as per the Content
Review Policy.

## Entry/Exit Policy and Archival Table

| S.No. | Content Element | Entry Policy | Exit Policy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | About Department | Whenever Department is realigned/changes its work distribution | Perpetual (10 years) since date of entry into archival |
| 2 | Programme/Schemes | Discontinuation of Sanction of Programme/Schemes for Central Sector, State Sector or both | Five (05) years since date of discontinuation |
| 3 | Policies | Discontinuation of Policy by Government — Central/State | Perpetual (10 years) since date of entry into archival |
| 4 | Acts/Rules | Issued through the gazette / Passed by the Central or State Government | Perpetual (10 years) — to be always available in the acts/rules database |
| 5 | Circulars/Notifications | Overruling Office Memorandum or Notification issued | Five (05) years since date of discontinuation |
| 6 | Documents/Publications/Reports | Completion of its validity period | Perpetual (10 years) since date of entry into archival |
| 7 | Directories | Not required | Not Applicable |
| 8 | What's New | As soon as it loses relevance | Automatically after expiry of the validity period |
| 9 | Tenders | As soon as it loses relevance | Five (05) years since date of discontinuation |
| 10 | Highlight | As soon as it loses relevance | Automatically after expiry of the validity period |
| 11 | Banners | As soon as it loses relevance | Automatically after expiry of the validity period |
| 12 | Photo-gallery | As soon as it loses relevance | Five (05) years since date of discontinuation |
| 13 | Group Wise Contents | As soon as it loses relevance | Five (05) years since date of discontinuation |
