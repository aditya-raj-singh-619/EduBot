# NIELIT — Copyright, Hyperlinking & Privacy Policies

## Copyright Policy

Material featured on this portal may be reproduced free of charge after
taking proper permission by sending a mail to NIELIT. However, the material
has to be reproduced accurately and must not be used in a derogatory manner
or in a misleading context. Wherever the material is published or issued to
others, the source must be prominently acknowledged.

The permission to reproduce this material does not extend to any material
identified as being copyright of a third party. Authorization to reproduce
such material must be obtained from the departments/copyright holders
concerned.

## Hyperlink Policy

### Links to External Websites/Portals

At many places on this website, you will find links to other
websites/portals. The links have been placed for your convenience. NIELIT is
not responsible for the contents and reliability of the linked websites and
does not necessarily endorse the views expressed in them. The mere presence
of a link or its listing on this portal should not be assumed as endorsement
of any kind. NIELIT cannot guarantee that these links will work all the time
and has no control over the availability of linked pages.

### Links to the NIELIT Website by Other Websites

NIELIT does not object to other websites linking directly to the
information hosted on this site, and no prior permission is required for
the same. However, NIELIT would like to be informed about any links
provided to this portal, so that the linking site can be informed of any
changes or updates. NIELIT does not permit its pages to be loaded into
frames on another site — pages belonging to this site must load into a
newly opened browser window of the user.

## Privacy Policy

The NIELIT website does not automatically capture any specific personal
information from visitors (such as name, phone number, or e-mail address)
that allows individual identification. If the NIELIT website requests
personal information, the visitor will be informed of the particular
purpose for which the information is gathered, and adequate security
measures will be taken to protect that personal information.

NIELIT does not sell or share any personally identifiable information
volunteered on the website to any third party (public/private). Any
information provided to the website is protected from loss, misuse,
unauthorized access or disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

NIELIT gathers certain information about visitors, such as Internet
Protocol (IP) addresses, domain name, browser type, operating system, and
the date, time, and pages visited. No attempt is made to link these
addresses to the identity of individuals visiting the site, unless an
attempt to damage the site has been detected.
